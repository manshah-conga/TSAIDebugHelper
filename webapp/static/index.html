LLM quota limits

Caps are counted in tokens, not currency, because Azure OpenAI does not report a per-call cost — a spend limit would never trigger. Leave a field blank for unlimited. Admins are never capped. A turn already running is allowed to finish, so an account can end up over its cap by at most one question.

Users

Verified is a quota decision, not a permission one: verifying an account moves it to the higher tier and grants it nothing else. Self-registered accounts start unverified. A per-user limit overrides the tier for that account only.
